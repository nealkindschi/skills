# Regional ATS Keyword Guide

Region-specific keyword strategies and compliance frameworks. Load when the user specifies a non-US region or when the job posting indicates a non-US location.

---

## United States (Default)

US-based companies typically deploy standard deterministic or AI-first ATS platforms without regionally-specific regulatory keyword requirements. However, certain industries demand specialized compliance keywords:

**Regulated Industries**:
- **Finance (FINRA/SEC)**: Series 7, Series 63, FINRA, SEC compliance, SOX, AML, KYC, Dodd-Frank
- **Healthcare (HIPAA)**: HIPAA compliance, HITECH, CMS, FDA regulations, CLIA, Joint Commission
- **Defense/Government (ITAR/FedRAMP)**: Security clearance (Secret, Top Secret), ITAR, FedRAMP, NIST, DFARS
- **Government Contracting**: FAR, DCAA, GSA Schedule, SCA, Davis-Bacon

**Key Regulatory Bodies**: EEOC, OFCCP — ATS systems at federal contractors must comply with affirmative action tracking. Including demographic signals is not required from the candidate side, but awareness of compliance frameworks helps understand the system's behavior.

## European Union (EU AI Act)

The EU AI Act classifies AI systems used for employment and recruitment as "high-risk" technologies. This has specific implications:

**ATS Implications**:
- Systems with greater transparency and explainability are preferred
- Emotion recognition technology in video screening is entirely forbidden
- Systems that infer sensitive demographic traits from biometric data are prohibited
- Predictive "social scoring" based on applicant behavior is banned
- GDPR applies — candidates have the right to meaningful information about the logic involved in automated decisions

**Keyword Strategy**:
- GDPR compliance terminology if the role involves data protection
- EU-specific regulatory frameworks relevant to the industry
- Explicit mention of language proficiencies using CEFR levels (B2, C1, C2)
- Degree equivalency notations if educated outside the EU (e.g., Bologna process alignment)
- Work authorization for the specific country (not just "EU")

## United Arab Emirates (UAE)

UAE-optimized ATS frameworks apply significant algorithmic weight to local regulatory terminology and sector-specific frameworks.

**High-Value UAE Keywords**:

| Category | Keywords | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory & Compliance | CBUAE Compliance, VAT & Corporate Tax, MOHRE Compliance | Demonstrates immediate operational capability within local legal frameworks |
| Government Initiatives | Emiratisation, Nafis, Vision 2031 Alignment | Aligns with state-mandated hiring quotas and national economic strategies |
| Jurisdictional Experience | DIFC & ADGM Experience, DAFZA & JAFZA, UAE Free Zone Operations | Indicates familiarity with specific economic zone regulations |
| Logistics & Administration | UAE Residence Visa Status, Emirates ID, UAE Driving Licence | Satisfies critical knockout logic regarding immediate employability |

These keywords must appear as plain text in the CV body to be successfully extracted and scored. Absence of these terms can severely depress a candidate's deterministic score even with extensive international experience.

## Other Regions (Strategy)

When the user specifies a region not covered above, apply this research framework:

1. Identify the region's major regulatory bodies and frameworks
2. Determine if there are state-mandated hiring initiatives or quotas
3. Check for region-specific professional certifications or licensing bodies
4. Identify any work authorization or visa terminology unique to that market
5. Note whether English is the primary business language or if bilingual keywords are expected

Common regional considerations:
- **Australia/New Zealand**: AHPRA (healthcare), ASIC (finance), NV1 security clearance, Fair Work framework
- **Canada**: PIPEDA, provincial regulatory bodies, NOC codes, bilingual (English/French) considerations for certain roles
- **Singapore**: MOM regulations, COMPASS framework, SkillsFuture, sector-specific statutory boards
- **India**: GST, RBI, SEBI, UGC/AICTE accreditation, PAN, Aadhaar (for identity verification roles)
- **UK**: FCA/PRA (finance), GMC/NMC (healthcare), SC/DV clearance, GDPR/UK GDPR, right to work and settled status
